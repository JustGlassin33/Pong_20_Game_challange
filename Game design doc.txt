Pong Game Design Document Outline



1. Title Page 
 Game Name – Pong
 Tag line – The same old paddle and ball computer game
 Team – Player 1, player 2 ,AI opponent 
 Date of last update
2. Gameplay – a ball is bounced back and forth by paddles on the left and right side of the screen. the top and bottom of screen have an invisible wall that deflects the ball
 Objectives – Get the ball past your opponents paddle to reach a score of 10 

5. Mechanics (Key Section) 
-Paddles that move up and down located on the far left and right of the screen
-Paddles should be locked to the view screen
-Ball starts in middle of screen and randomly chooses to move left or right  to start the game
-Ball bounces off surfaces/paddles at an angle
-Ball speed increases with each paddle bounce
-When the ball passes by one of the paddles a scoreboard at the top of the screen will update
-When a score of ten is reached the game will end and declair the person (or Ai) with 10 points the winner
